INTRODUCTION

BEHIND DOMIX

DOMIX is a 17-year-old DJ and producer from Lower Austria. Active since 2024, he has progressed from local performances to festival stages, larger events, sponsored appearances and his first radio set on ENERGY Extravadance. His identity is built around energetic performances, professional preparation and a strong connection with the crowd.

His sets combine high energy, custom mashups, recognizable tracks and spontaneous interaction. While DJing remains his main focus, DOMIX continues to develop as a producer and shape an increasingly individual sound.

THE SOUND

MUSIC &
PERFORMANCE

DOMIX builds his sound around Tech House, Techno and Drum & Bass. Rather than following a fixed formula, each set is adapted to the time slot, setting and audience. Strong transitions, custom mashups, recognizable moments and controlled genre changes build energy while keeping the crowd engaged.

On stage, he combines technical focus with direct interaction, using the microphone selectively and reading the room to create a performance rather than simply playing tracks.
